HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY DOCUMENT

--------------------------------------------------
1. POLICY OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------
This Health Insurance Policy provides financial protection against medical expenses incurred due to illness, injury, or hospitalization. The policy covers inpatient treatment, day care procedures, and certain pre- and post-hospitalization expenses, subject to the terms and conditions mentioned herein.

--------------------------------------------------
2. SUM INSURED
--------------------------------------------------
The total sum insured under this policy is INR 5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakhs Only) per policy year.

--------------------------------------------------
3. ELIGIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------
- Minimum entry age: 18 years
- Maximum entry age: 65 years
- Dependent children are covered from 3 months to 25 years of age.

--------------------------------------------------
4. COVERAGE DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------
The policy covers the following medical expenses:

4.1 Hospitalization Expenses
- Room rent, nursing charges, and ICU expenses
- Surgeon, anesthetist, and consultant fees
- Medicines and consumables

4.2 Pre-Hospitalization
- Medical expenses incurred up to 30 days before hospitalization

4.3 Post-Hospitalization
- Medical expenses incurred up to 60 days after discharge

4.4 Day Care Procedures
- Coverage for procedures requiring hospitalization for less than 24 hours such as cataract surgery, chemotherapy, and dialysis

--------------------------------------------------
5. EXCLUSIONS
--------------------------------------------------
The policy does not cover the following:

- Pre-existing diseases during the first 36 months
- Cosmetic or aesthetic treatments
- Dental treatment unless arising from an accident
- Injuries due to self-inflicted harm
- Treatment for substance abuse or alcohol-related conditions

--------------------------------------------------
6. WAITING PERIOD
--------------------------------------------------
- Initial waiting period of 30 days for all illnesses except accidents
- Specific diseases have a waiting period of 24 months
- Pre-existing diseases have a waiting period of 36 months

--------------------------------------------------
7. CASHLESS FACILITY
--------------------------------------------------
Cashless hospitalization is available at network hospitals recognized by SecureLife Health Insurance Co., subject to prior approval.

--------------------------------------------------
8. CLAIM PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------
8.1 Cashless Claims
- Inform the insurer 48 hours before planned hospitalization
- Submit pre-authorization request at the network hospital

8.2 Reimbursement Claims
- Submit claim form within 15 days of discharge
- Attach original bills, discharge summary, and medical reports

--------------------------------------------------
9. RENEWAL
--------------------------------------------------
The policy can be renewed annually. Continuous renewal ensures continuity benefits such as waiting period credits.

--------------------------------------------------
10. CANCELLATION
--------------------------------------------------
The insurer may cancel the policy in case of fraud, misrepresentation, or non-disclosure of material facts.
