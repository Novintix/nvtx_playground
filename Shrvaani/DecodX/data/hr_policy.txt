DecodX Global Operations - Human Resources Policy (v2026.1)

1. RECRUITMENT AND CANDIDATE EXPERIENCE
1.1 Equal Opportunity Employment
DecodX is an equal opportunity employer. We prohibit discrimination and harassment of any kind based on race, color, sex, religion, sexual orientation, national origin, disability, genetic information, pregnancy, or any other protected characteristic as outlined by federal, state, or local laws.

1.2 Candidate Privacy Policy
Personal data provided by candidates during the application process is handled in accordance with global data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA). Data is used solely for recruitment purposes and retained for a maximum of 24 months unless explicit consent is provided for longer retention.

1.3 Interview Reimbursement
Candidates traveling more than 50 miles for on-site interviews are eligible for travel expense reimbursement. 
- Airfare: Economy class only, booked at least 7 days in advance.
- Lodging: Standard room at preferred corporate hotel partners.
- Meals: Maximum daily allowance of $75.
All receipts must be submitted via the Candidate Portal within 10 business days of the interview.

1.4 Background Checks
All offers of employment are contingent upon the successful completion of a comprehensive background check, including criminal history, education verification, and previous employment verification.

2. EMPLOYEE BENEFITS AND COMPENSATION
2.1 Health and Wellness
- Medical/Dental/Vision: Effective from Day 1 of employment.
- Mental Health Support: 12 complimentary sessions annually via our Employee Assistance Program (EAP).

2.2 Paid Time Off (PTO)
- Annual Leave: 20 days per calendar year, accrued monthly.
- Sick Leave: 10 days per calendar year.
- Public Holidays: 12 fixed holidays based on regional office location.

2.3 Remote Work Policy
DecodX operates on a Hybrid-First model. Employees are expected to be in the office 2 days per week (Tuesday and Thursday) unless a "Remote-Full" contract is explicitly negotiated at the time of hire.

3. CODE OF CONDUCT
3.1 Professionalism
Employees must maintain a high standard of professional conduct. Any form of workplace bullying, harassment, or retaliation is strictly prohibited.

3.2 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any external business interests or relationships that may conflict with the interests of DecodX. High-level officers are prohibited from serving on external corporate boards without Board of Directors approval.
