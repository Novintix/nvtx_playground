DecodX Global Operations - Commercial Sales and Discounting Policy (Corporate Governance v4.2)

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This policy defines the standard operating procedures (SOPs) for revenue recognition, discounting authorities, and commercial engagement across all global regions (North, South, East, West).

2. STANDARD DISCOUNTING STRUCTURE
2.1 Tier 1: Automated Authority (0% - 10%)
- Account Executives (AEs) are authorized to apply discounts up to 10% without additional managerial oversight for standard contracts.
- Condition: Contract duration must be at least 12 months.

2.2 Tier 2: Management Approval (10.1% - 15%)
- Requires written approval from the Regional Sales Director.
- Condition: Must include a "Business Rationalization Note" (BRN) explaining competitive threats or strategic value.

2.3 Tier 3: Executive/CFO Approval (15.1% - 25%)
- Reserved for strategic enterprise deals. 
- Requires approval from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and the Head of Global Sales.
- Condition: Quarterly limit of 3 such deals per region.

2.4 Prohibited Discounts (> 25%)
- Discounts exceeding 25% are strictly prohibited under current revenue recognition guidelines (GAAP/IFRS compliance).

3. REGIONAL MARKET EXCEPTIONS
3.1 Highly Competitive Markets (South, East)
- The "South" and "East" regions are designated as Stage 1 Competitive Zones.
- Temporary Flex: Regional Managers in these zones may authorize up to 12% at the AE level for "Tier 1" products during Q3 and Q4.

3.2 Emerging Markets (West)
- Standard Tier 1 rules apply. No regional flex is authorized at this time.

4. PRODUCT SPECIFIC GUIDELINES
4.1 Product-A (Flagship)
- Volume-based strategy. Standard rules apply.
- Bundle Discount: An additional 2% may be applied if bundled with Product-C.

4.2 Product-B (Premium Service)
- High-margin product. Max discount capped at 5% regardless of authority levels.

4.3 Product-C (Legacy/Clearance)
- End-of-life status. Clearance discounts up to 30% are permitted ONLY with VP of Finance approval and must be recognized as non-recurring revenue.

5. ENGAGEMENT AND LOGISTICS
5.1 Deal Value Thresholds
- Deals > $500,000 (USD): Require mandatory Legal review.
- Deals > $1,000,000 (USD): Require mandatory Finance Committee review.

5.2 Turnaround Times
- Management Approval (Tier 2): 48 business hours.
- Executive Approval (Tier 3): 5-7 business days.

6. COMPLIANCE AND AUDIT
6.1 Monthly Review
The Corporate Strategy and Audit team will perform monthly reconciliations of all applied discounts.
6.2 Non-Compliance
Repeated unauthorized discounting will result in disciplinary action, including potential forfeiture of sales commissions for the participating unit.
