Sales & Discount Policy – Version 3.2

1. Discount Limits
- Maximum standard discount allowed: 10%
- Discounts above 10% require approval from Regional Sales Head.
- Discounts above 15% are not permitted under any circumstances.

2. Regional Performance Guidelines
- High-competition regions (South, East) may request temporary discount flexibility.
- Any flexibility beyond standard limits must be reviewed quarterly.

3. Approval Process
- Deals exceeding ₹5,00,000 in value require finance approval.
- Approval turnaround time should not exceed 3 business days.

4. Policy Review History
- In 2023, discount limits in South region were temporarily increased to 12%.
- During this period, sales volume increased by approximately 9%.
- The policy reverted to 10% in early 2024.

5. Compliance Notes
- Repeated breaches of discount policy must be escalated.
- Policy violations are reviewed during quarterly audits.
