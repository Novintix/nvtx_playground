Sales & Discount Policy – Version 4.0

1. Discount Limits
- Maximum standard discount allowed: 10%
- Discounts above 15% are STRICTLY PROHIBITED and are considered a compliance violation.

2. Regional Exceptions
- "South" Region is classified as High Competition.
- Exception: South Region is permitted up to 12% discount to maintain market share.
- "East" Region has NO exceptions. Standard 10% limit applies.

3. Product-Specific Rules
- Product-A: Focus on volume. Max discount 12% allowed globally (supersedes Regional limit).
- Product-B: Premium product. Max discount 5% only.
- Product-C: End-of-life. Clearance discounts up to 25% allowed ONLY with CFO approval.

4. Approval Authorities
- < 10% : Auto-approved
- 10% - 15% : Regional Manager Approval
- > 15% : CFO Approval Required
- > ₹50 Lakhs Deal Value : Finance Committee Approval

5. Review History
- 2024: South region sales dropped despite 12% discounts.
- 2025: East region showing signs of unauthorized discounting (16-20%).
